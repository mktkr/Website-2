If you own a home in Arizona, have minor children, or have assets you want to pass on to your family — you've probably wondered whether you need a living trust.

The short answer: most Arizona families benefit significantly from a [revocable living trust](/services/wills-and-trusts). Here's why.

## Arizona Probate Is Public and Expensive

When you pass away with only a will (or no estate plan at all), your estate must go through probate — Arizona's court-supervised process for distributing your assets. This process is public, meaning anyone can see what you owned and who inherited it. It typically takes 6–12 months and can cost your family thousands in attorney fees and court costs.

## A Trust Avoids Probate Entirely

A revocable living trust holds your assets during your lifetime and transfers them directly to your beneficiaries when you pass — without any court involvement. It's private, fast, and dramatically less expensive for your family.

## When a Will Is Sufficient

Not everyone needs a trust. If you're young, rent your home, and have minimal assets, a simple will combined with powers of attorney and [healthcare directives](/services/healthcare-directive) may be all you need right now. As your life changes — buying a home, having children, building savings — you can add a trust later.
